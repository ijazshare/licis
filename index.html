2. Mission (pick-one)

  1. LICIS unites Long Island's imams and scholars to provide consistent religious guidance, support one another, and serve the Muslim community with wisdom and integrity.
  2. LICIS exists to coordinate Long Island's Muslim religious leadership, offering unified guidance, mentorship, and a collective voice for the community's spiritual needs.
  3. LICIS strengthens the Muslim community of Long Island by convening imams and scholars for shared learning, guidance, and coordinated communal response.

3. Vision (pick-one)

  1. A united, respected body of Islamic scholarship serving every Long Island Muslim community.
  2. Long Island's imams and scholars, unified in guidance, trusted across faiths.
  3. One council, many communities: shared wisdom guiding Long Island's Muslims.

4. Core purposes (for Certificate of Incorporation)

  • Foster unity, coordination, and mutual support among imams and Islamic scholars on Long Island
  • Provide consistent religious guidance and rulings to member mosques and the broader Muslim community
  • Represent Long Island Muslims in interfaith and civic engagement
  • Mentor and develop emerging imams and scholars
  • Convene collective scholarly consultation (shura) on community religious matters
  • Coordinate rapid, unified response during community crises (deaths, disasters, public incidents)
  • Educate the public on Islamic teachings and promote religious literacy

5. Sample by-laws (skeleton)

I. Name & Offices

  • Name: Long Island Council of Imams & Scholars, Inc. (LICIS)
  • Principal office: Nassau or Suffolk County, NY
  • Board may add other offices

II. Purposes

  • Religious, charitable, educational (IRC 501(c)(3))
  • Coordination among LI imams/scholars; religious guidance; interfaith/civic relations; community education
  • No private benefit to members

III. Membership

  • Eligibility: imams/scholars serving LI masajid or Islamic institutions credential/recommendation criteria TBD
  • Categories: Voting Member; Associate (non-voting: students of knowledge, chaplains, retired)
  • Dues: none
  • Admission: majority board vote
  • Removal: two-thirds board vote after notice and hearing

IV. Meetings of Members

  • Annual meeting: each fall to elect directors
  • Special meetings: called by Chair, board, or 10% of members
  • Quorum: one-third of voting members
  • Voting: majority of votes cast; one vote per member; proxies not allowed
  • Remote participation allowed (N-PCL 603/605)

V. Board of Directors

  • Size: 7 (range 5-9)
  • Terms: 2 yrs, staggered; limit 3 consecutive terms
  • Election: by members at annual meeting
  • Vacancies: filled by board until next annual meeting
  • Removal: two-thirds of members, or of board for cause
  • Quorum: majority; action without meeting only by unanimous written or electronic consent

VI. Officers

  • Chair/President, Vice Chair, Secretary, Treasurer
  • Elected by board; 1-yr term, max 3 consecutive
  • Chair: presides, is the official spokesperson; Secretary: minutes and records; Treasurer: finances and reports
  • Secretary and Chair cannot be the same person (N-PCL 713)

VII. Committees

  • Executive: officers; acts between board meetings, within limits
  • Religious Affairs/Fatwa: shared positions (moon sighting, rulings, and so on); consensus, or two-thirds
  • Community Relations: interfaith, government, media
  • Finance/Audit: budget and controls (audit committee rules apply above the N-PCL revenue threshold)
  • Ad hoc committees as needed

VIII. Shura / Decision-Making

  • Aim for consensus through shura before any vote
  • Religious positions: issued only with Religious Affairs approval and a board vote
  • No member speaks for LICIS without authorization

IX. Conflict of Interest & Whistleblower

  • Written policy required (N-PCL 715-a); whistleblower policy (715-b) if thresholds met
  • Annual disclosure statements
  • Interested director does not deliberate or vote

X. Finances

  • Fiscal year: Jan 1-Dec 31
  • Annual budget approved by board
  • Two signatures above $1,000
  • Annual financial report to members

XI. Indemnification

  • Indemnify directors/officers to the extent N-PCL allows
  • D&O insurance recommended

XII. General Provisions

  • Non-discrimination in programs, within the organization's religious scope
  • 501(c)(3) limits: no endorsing or opposing candidates; lobbying stays insubstantial
  • Dissolution: assets go to another 501(c)(3) with similar purposes; no member receives assets
  • Amendments: two-thirds of the board, ratified by majority of members; 14 days' written notice

6. Registration checklist (NY)

  • Search name availability (NY DOS); consider a name reservation
  • File Certificate of Incorporation (DOS-1511), charitable type, with 501(c)(3) purpose and dissolution clauses
  • Get an EIN (IRS, online)
  • Hold the organizational meeting: adopt by-laws and COI policy, elect directors and officers
  • NY Charities Bureau: register (CHAR410), or claim the religious-organization exemption
  • IRS: file Form 1023 (or 1023-EZ if eligible)
  • NYS sales tax exemption (ST-119.2) after IRS determination
  • Open a bank account (EIN, certificate, board resolution)
  • Get insurance: general liability, D&O
  • Annual filings: CHAR500 (if registered), 990/990-N, DOS biennial statement

Sample only. Review with a NY nonprofit attorney before adopting.
